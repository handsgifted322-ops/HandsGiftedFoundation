# Hands Gifted Skills — Public Architecture

## Governing public model

Hands Gifted is a faith-centered child and family development platform.

The public experience is organized around five connected layers:

1. Children & learning
2. Hands Gifted Skills
3. Family and community resources
4. Original Hands Gifted products
5. Direct Hands Gifted services

## Hands Gifted Skills

The four public skill worlds are:

- Hands Gifted Cooking
- Hands Gifted Gardening
- Hands Gifted Braiding
- Hands Gifted Sewing

These are one connected Hands Gifted system, not four separate businesses.

Each skill world uses the pattern:

**Learn → Practice → Book / Request → Shop**

Each also connects to children/family development and a relevant Scripture study trail.

## Free / paid boundary

Public information should provide real value, practical introductory guidance, Scripture connections, and trustworthy resource links.

Complete original Hands Gifted creations—books, workbooks, planners, kits, lesson collections, systems, courses, member resources, and proprietary processes—belong in the product or paid-depth layer when they are complete.

## Service readiness

A skill may belong in the Hands Gifted service system without every possible service inside that skill being available today.

- Braiding: direct appointment inquiries can be accepted, subject to confirmation of style, price, preparation, timing, deposit terms, and availability.
- Cooking: service world; exact public food/cooking offers must meet applicable food-safety and legal requirements before being advertised as available.
- Gardening: service world; exact service menu should grow from demonstrated capability and should not imply advanced landscaping/horticultural expertise that has not been established.
- Sewing: service world; selected basic work can be considered within demonstrated capability, while full garment construction must not be represented as available until that skill is established.

## Privacy boundary

Public skill content may be informed by real household practice, but protected child records, health information, finances, legal matters, addresses, journals, school records, and private household administration do not belong on the public website.
