# Hands Gifted — Original Digital Content & Intellectual Property Standard
Version: 2026-10-13 (internal draft)

## Scope
Applies to public website copy, Academy materials, free resources, worksheets, workbooks, journals, planners, illustrations, videos, recipes, product listings, and digital downloads.

## Required checks before publication
1. Develop original expression and practical exercises for Hands Gifted; do not copy another creator's products, layouts, illustrations, branded language, or proprietary resources.
2. Distinguish uncopyrightable ideas, facts, methods, and general skills from protected expression. Independently write and design the finished work.
3. Record the source and license for every third-party photo, font, icon, music clip, template, software package, or excerpt. Confirm redistribution and commercial-use rights where applicable.
4. Review AI-assisted output for substantial similarity, inaccuracies, trademark conflicts, and meaningful human creative contribution. Do not claim exclusive copyright over unprotectable AI-only output.
5. Maintain a content record: owner, author/editor, date, version, source files, permissions, review outcome, and release location.
6. Keep private household, journal, children's records, and other personal data out of public materials unless separately and explicitly authorized.
7. Confirm accessibility, usability, link/download function, price/free status, and editorial accuracy.
8. Do not publish until the content owner approves and licensing/privacy checks pass.

## Routing
- GitHub: website source, version history, public resource code, and this development standard.
- Hands Gifted Drive SOPs: approved operating procedures and editorial/checklist masters.
- Product library: final masters, exports, cover art, and license evidence.
- Private Family Command Center: family-specific prototypes and source observations, not public-facing records.

This document is an internal operating standard, not legal advice or a claim that every asset is copyrightable.
