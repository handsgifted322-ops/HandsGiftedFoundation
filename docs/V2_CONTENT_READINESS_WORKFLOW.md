# Hands Gifted V2 — Content Readiness & Publication Workflow

## Purpose

Hands Gifted separates **creating something** from **being ready to publish it**. Ideas, research, images, lessons, products, family practices, and drafts may be valuable before they are public-ready.

The default rule is: **creation is private until deliberately promoted.**

## Workflow

`CAPTURED → DEVELOPING → ORGANIZED → VERIFY → APPROVAL → PUBLISH_READY → PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED`

### 1. CAPTURED
Raw idea, image, note, research question, lesson concept, product idea, or observation. No public promise is implied.

### 2. DEVELOPING
The idea is actively being researched, written, designed, tested, or connected to a Hands Gifted project.

### 3. ORGANIZED
Supporting material is together: copy, images, sources, links, project context, audience, and intended use.

### 4. VERIFY
Check factual claims, citations, Scripture references, permissions/ownership, links, privacy, product/program availability, and whether the material accurately represents current Hands Gifted capability.

### 5. APPROVAL
Founder/authorized reviewer decides whether the item should move forward. Approval is distinct from technical completion.

### 6. PUBLISH_READY
Final copy/assets are complete; intended audience/channel is known; privacy and accuracy checks passed. This means the item *may* be published, not that it must be.

### 7. PUBLISHED
Record publication channel, date, URL/reference, final asset/copy, and related campaign/project.

### 8. ARCHIVED
Preserve the final item and evidence/history without keeping it in the active publishing queue.

## Access boundary

Content has a separate visibility scope:

- `PERSONAL` — founder/private workspace.
- `FAMILY` — approved household members according to role.
- `PUBLIC` — visitors, customers, organizations, B2C/B2B/B2G audiences.
- `SHARED_KNOWLEDGE_EVIDENCE` — reusable source/evidence layer with its own authorization.

**Readiness does not grant visibility.** A PUBLISH_READY item is not public until explicitly approved for PUBLIC scope and actually published.

## Business priority is separate

Existing business priority states remain:

- `ACTIVE BUSINESS`
- `INTERNAL R&D`
- `FUTURE ROADMAP`

These describe business maturity/priority. They do not determine content visibility or publication readiness.

## Public-release gate

Before public release, confirm:

- Intended audience and purpose are clear.
- Claims and citations are verified where applicable.
- Scripture/faith interpretation is distinguishable from documented historical or factual claims.
- Private household, child, school, medical, financial, journal, case-management, or administrative information is removed unless explicitly authorized and appropriate.
- Images/assets are owned, licensed, generated for use, or otherwise permitted.
- Products/programs/services are described according to their real current status.
- Links and calls to action work.
- Final copy and asset are preserved as evidence.
- Founder/authorized approval is recorded.

## V2 product behavior

Personal dashboard should provide a **Content Readiness** queue with counts by stage and a focused "What needs to become ready?" view.

Research/Teach Me items begin in PERSONAL by default. A research collection may later produce a FAMILY lesson/activity or a PUBLIC resource, but those are deliberate promotions rather than automatic exposure.

Public routes query only explicitly public, approved material. Draft/development records never become public merely because they exist in the database.

## Operating principle

> Create freely. Organize deliberately. Verify before claiming. Publish intentionally. Preserve the evidence.
