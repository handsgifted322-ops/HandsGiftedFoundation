# Pillar Four — Walk According to the Most High

**Status:** Approved direction; internal development draft, not published.

**Series:** A New Walk, One Step at a Time.

## Public placement
- Faith & Biblical Living > A New Walk, One Step at a Time
- Free Resource Library > Bible Study (beginner lessons, glossary, activities)
- Women & Daughters > Becoming a Daughter of Sarah (optional specialized pathway)

## Audience and approach
Welcome first-time Bible readers, returning learners, and people exploring a new way of life. Never assume prior knowledge of scripture, dietary laws, Sabbath, modesty, or commandments. Avoid framing learning stages as spiritual rankings. Keep the brand pillars unchanged.

## Learning areas
1. Start Reading the Bible
2. A New Beginning
3. Women of Scripture
4. Understanding Biblical Living
5. Living What I Learn

## Proposed free lesson sequence
1. How Do I Begin Reading the Bible? — 2 Timothy 3:16–17
2. What Does It Mean to Seek the Most High? — Isaiah 55:6–7
3. Can I Change the Way I Live? — Romans 12:2
4. Who Was Sarah? — Genesis 18:9–15; 1 Peter 3:5–6
5. Why Does the Bible Speak About Commandments? — Deuteronomy 6:5–9
6. How Can I Practice What I Learn? — James 1:22–25

## Lesson template
Beginner question → scripture (clearly separated) → context → attributed interpretation → reflection → practical activity → resources → next free lesson. Adapt for daughters and adults. Prefer text and accessible images first; videos later when useful.

## Safeguards
- Preserve founder's private journal, family circumstances, and personal testimony; **do not publish** without separate explicit approval.
- Verify KJV edition and any licensing, references, and theological attribution. Distinguish scriptural text from Israelite/IUIC-aligned interpretation; do not imply organizational affiliation.
- Use original lessons and worksheets; link to external resources instead of copying them.
- Do not claim lessons, workbooks, checkout, or services are live until verified.
- Public publication requires editorial review and explicit merge/deploy approval.

## First release scope
One landing page, one complete free beginner Bible reading lesson, short glossary, and starter worksheet. Expand only after review and feedback.
