# Hands Gifted Public Surface Hierarchy

The public website is a community-facing Hands Gifted resource, learning, relationship, and commerce surface. It is separate from the owner’s Command Center and from private family/application data.

## Core public entry points

1. **Home** — clear Hands Gifted story, current value, key resources, current offers, and B2C/B2B routing.
2. **Explore / Resource Center** — searchable, interconnected topic pathways that let visitors continue deeper through related guidance and resources.
3. **Family & Household** — practical household systems, routines, planning, food/inventory, organization, and family-life resources appropriate for public use.
4. **Children & Learning** — public parent-guided learning, practical skills, creativity, and family learning information without exposing private child records.
5. **Faith & Scripture** — in-depth Scripture-connected learning and practical application; faith should be structural rather than decorative.
6. **Practical Skills** — approved public learning around cooking, sewing, gardening, natural hair/self-care, creative work, and other developed skills, labeled by actual readiness.
7. **Family Stability Resources** — verified public information and resource navigation without presenting Hands Gifted as a licensed social-service provider unless that changes.
8. **Household Operating System** — the current flagship commercial pathway, presented accurately according to its actual stage.
9. **Products / Shop** — only approved products or clearly status-labeled concepts; database existence does not equal availability.
10. **For Organizations / Partnerships** — B2B pathway for organizations, businesses, providers, educators, collaborators, vendors, and appropriate partners.
11. **About** — founder/business purpose, mission, public story, and link to Follow My Journey without exposing private family crises.
12. **Follow My Journey** — public founder/business build updates, lessons, milestones, process notes, testing, and appropriate reflections; not a private diary.
13. **Contact** — general inquiries, B2C contact, B2B partnership contact, and approved next actions.
14. **Member / Family Login** — entry point to authenticated systems; protected content remains outside the public surface.

## Audience rule

- **B2C:** active public audience.
- **B2B:** active public audience.
- **B2G:** undecided/future. Do not publish government contracting, procurement, agency-service, or capability claims until the owner explicitly approves a B2G strategy.

## Publishing rule

Public pages should provide substantial free value and help visitors understand Hands Gifted. Complete proprietary systems, structured workbooks, lessons, memberships, tools, and paid resources may remain protected or commercial when genuinely developed.

Only intentionally approved public content may move onto the website. Private household, child, school, medical, financial, legal, journal, Command Center, private Academy, and internal business records remain protected.
